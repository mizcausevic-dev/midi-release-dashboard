# Chord shells can leave room for the rest of a track

Three notes can carry a chord's identity without filling every octave. A root, third and seventh can establish a seventh chord while leaving a producer free to choose the bass register and add an extension. A rootless third, seventh and ninth can do a similar job when the bass supplies the root.

The Jazz House Chords collection uses a three-note rootless dominant-ninth shell. Lo-Fi Hip-Hop Chords uses minor-seventh shells. These are starting points: a voicing that works alone may conflict with the bass or melody once a full arrangement is playing.

Start by loading a chord clip and a bass patch. Move the chord up an octave if it crowds the low end. Then try changing one note or its timing. Keep the version that supports the song, not the version that looks most complex in a piano roll.

The MIDI files contain notes and timing, not an instrument sound. Their previews use a simple synthesizer and are not masters. Listen in the intended arrangement before making a production decision.
