## What it is, three ways

- **For a producer:** Editable chord, bass, melody and drum ideas to adapt in a DAW.
- **For a collaborator:** Combined studies and separate parts that make rearrangement and sound selection explicit.
- **For Miz:** A documented catalog of product candidates, each with a public preview and technical release evidence. Commercial readiness still depends on listening and rights review.

## How it works

Open a pack page, hear its simple-synth preview, and download the ZIP. Choose the combined arrangement or separate roles; using both together doubles notes. The event catalog, checksums and validation report document the exported files. Preview videos animate score events over generated art; they are not recorded performances or masters.
